# When you look up at the sky

When you look up at the sky, all you can see are clouds and stars, right? But beyond them, there's the Earth's atmosphere surrounding our planet, an invisible layer that reaches nearly 10,000km above the Earth. This atmosphere protects the Earth from many dangers coming from outer space, but could I have told you this in the seventh century? Of course not, because this wasn't discovered until the 17th century and this is where the miracle becomes clear.

# وَجَعَلْنَا السَّمَٓاءَ سَقْفاً مَحْفُوظا

And we made the sky a protected ceiling. So the Quran was pointing to this reality, centuries before modern science discovered it.
